### About me

I'm an independent builder focused on turning ideas into real products.

I'm interested in the full journey: finding a problem, designing the experience, building the technology and continuously improving the product.

Currently building and exploring:

- **WOY Marketplace** — a peer-to-peer marketplace focused on making local buying and selling simple, fast and accessible.
- **Gabriel** — an experimental AI project exploring financial markets, economic information, prediction and continuous evaluation.
- **Project X** — a long-term exploration around realistic 3D assets and digital representations.

I like working across product, UI/UX, software, AI and entrepreneurship rather than staying inside a single discipline.
